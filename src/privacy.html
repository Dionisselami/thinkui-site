Privacy

Short version: we collect what is needed to run your account and meter your usage. Your code and your prompts are not part of it.

Last updated {{YEAR}}.

What we collect

  • Account details, the email address you sign up with, your name if you give one, and a hashed password or the identity your OAuth provider returns.
  • Billing details, handled by our payment processor. We receive the outcome of a payment and the last four digits of a card; we never receive or store the card itself.
  • Usage counts, how many tool calls your key has made in a period, and which tools were called, so we can meter plans and show you your own usage.

What we do not collect

  • Your source code, your files, or the contents of your project.
  • Your prompts or your agent's conversation history. The server runs locally inside your client; the query string that reaches us is what the tool call itself carries.
  • Advertising or cross-site tracking data. There are no ad networks on this site.

Cookies

We use the minimum needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms against abuse. There are no third-party advertising cookies on this site, and there are no third-party requests on it either: the fonts this site uses are served from our own domain, so loading a page contacts nobody but us.

Who we share it with

Our hosting provider and our payment processor, to the extent each needs it to do its job. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account and usage records are kept while your account is open and for a short period afterwards, so that billing and refund questions can be settled. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove what we are not required to retain for tax or accounting purposes.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it or to delete your account.
  • Cancel a paid plan at any time from your account page.

Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will act on the request.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will tell account holders by email before the change takes effect.
