1. A reference, with its reasoning

The question you would otherwise answer by opening ten apps. This is the whole first result; the second one is named below it.

find_ui_references(query="how a paywall frames its free trial")

Trimmed: the second result's body, and nothing else. Note what the second hit is — a productivity app, not a paywall. The tool returns its nearest match and names it, rather than pretending every hit is a good one.

Two things to notice. The first result is a flow, not a screenshot: it is indexed by the decision it demonstrates. And the second result is a weak match that the ranking still surfaced — so you can discount it, but you are not told a lie by omission.

What this is not

These are evidence, not templates. The server tells your agent as much on every call: use one to settle the decision it answers, then design for the product in hand. Copying a reference's layout is both a licence problem and a bad idea — the point is the reasoning, not the pixels.
