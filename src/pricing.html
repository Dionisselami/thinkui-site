Do I have to subscribe?

You can pay monthly or once. Starter is $7.99 a month and Pro is $15.99 — both monthly, both cancel in one click, so the cheapest way to try it is one month of Starter. If you would rather pay once, Lifetime is $152 and never renews.

What counts as a tool call?

One request from your agent to the server, one search, one component fetch, one icon lookup. Reading the results does not cost anything extra. Each request your client makes to the server counts once, whatever it comes back with.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes on every plan. Every result identifies where it came from, so your own records stay in order when you hand work over.

How do refunds and cancellation work?

Cancel in one click from your account page. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and cancelling stops the next charge.
