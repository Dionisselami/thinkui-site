Do I need a paid plan to try it?

No. The free tier gives you all five tools and 200 calls a month, which is enough to see whether your agent uses them well. Upgrade whenever it starts carrying real work.

What counts as a tool call?

One request from your agent to the server, one search, one component fetch, one icon lookup. Reading the results does not cost anything extra, and a refused query is not billed.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes on Pro and Team. Every result identifies where it came from, so your own records stay in order when you hand work over.

How do refunds and cancellation work?

Cancel in one click from your account page. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and cancelling stops the next charge.
