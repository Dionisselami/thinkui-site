Do I have to subscribe?

Not to try it. A free account gets 12 tool calls in total, on every tool, with no card. After that, Starter is $7.99 a month and Pro is $15.99 — both 20% off, both cancel in one click. A card is required at checkout. Lifetime is $152 once.

What counts as a tool call?

One request from your agent to the server, one search, one component fetch, one icon lookup. Connecting, listing the tools and reading the results do not count. Each tool your agent runs counts once, whatever it comes back with. Paid plans are unlimited; the free 12 are counted this way.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes on every plan. Every result identifies where it came from, so your own records stay in order when you hand work over.

How do refunds and cancellation work?

Cancel in one click from your account page. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and cancelling stops the next charge. Partial periods are not refunded, with one exception: if the server returns nothing useful in your first week, email support@thinkui.xyz within 7 days of your first paid charge and we refund that first month.
